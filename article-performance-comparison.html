6

Measurement notes

  • What these numbers are: Lighthouse simulates a mid-range phone on a slow 4G connection. They indicate the real experience but do not replace field data from real visitors.
  • Image cache: the new site's mobile figures were taken with its image cache warm, as the CDN keeps it in production. On a picture's first request, while it was still being cut to size, the new article scored 89–92 (LCP 3.3–3.8 s); still well ahead of the old site.
  • The final fair comparison should be run on staging, with ads and GTM enabled on the new site.
  • Field data from real visitors (CrUX and Search Console) will be available about 28 days after launch.
7

Summary

On mobile, the new article page:

  • Shows its main image 8× faster: 2.4 s instead of 19.5 s.
  • Is ready for interaction in 60 ms instead of 1.5 s.
  • Is about 7× lighter: 551 KB instead of 3.75 MB.
  • Does not move while loading: CLS 0, against 0.62 on the old site's desktop.
The new article page passes every Core Web Vitals threshold on mobile and desktop. The old one fails LCP, TBT and CLS on mobile, and LCP and CLS on desktop.
